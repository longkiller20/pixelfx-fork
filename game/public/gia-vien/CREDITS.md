# Gia Viên: where its pictures come from

`khoi.png` is cut by `tools/gia-vien-khoi.py` from tile sheets the game already has. All of it is **CC0** (public domain).

| Tiles | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| Ván Gỗ, Thùng Gỗ, Đá Xám, Đá Cát, Thùng Rượu, Rương, Gỗ Tròn | *old frogatto tile art*, OpenGameArt (https://opengameart.org/content/old-frogatto-tile-art), via https://github.com/Tiddybub/2d-assets | CC0 |
| Đá Xanh, Đất Nung, Vân Đài, Vách Đá Xanh, Vách Đất Nung | *Xianxia Pixel Pack v2.1* (`public/xianxia/LICENSE.txt`) | CC0 |
| Ngói and Hiên (grey, blue, red, jade) | *Oriental Roof Tiles*, OpenGameArt (https://opengameart.org/content/oriental-roof-tiles), via https://github.com/Tiddybub/2d-assets | CC0 |

Everything else in the Gia Viên is drawn in code with pixelfx (`../src/fx/farm/`, `../src/fx/home/`): the hens, ducks,
pigs and cows, the four herbs and their growth, the soil beds, fences, troughs, coop, haystack and signs, and the
furniture there was no picture for (doors, windows, tables, chairs, beds, shelves, vases, scrolls, paper and wood walls,
bamboo, wooden platforms, lanterns, the alchemy furnace).
